# Security Policy

## Posture

Open source. The **VAKED** token contract is source-verified on
[Sourcify](https://repo.sourcify.dev/contracts/full_match/137/0x2Ae7DA713A2c8527AF70825C0F79632AF2e2ae4A/)
(exact match) and ownership is renounced — the contract is immutable: no
admin key, no pause, no upgrade path.

## Responsible disclosure

- Report vulnerabilities to **peter.lodri@gmail.com**.
- Do not exploit a confirmed issue beyond what is needed to demonstrate it.
- Do not disclose publicly before we have had a chance to respond.
- A reasonable window (e.g. 7 days) before public disclosure is appreciated.

## Scope

- The **entheai** Worker and Redis gateway (license verification, release
  distribution).
- The **VAKED** token contract.
- The deploy/signing tooling.
- The sites.

## Bug bounty

- **In scope**: anything listed above.
- **Out of scope**: denial-of-service and social engineering.
- No formal payout yet, but genuine findings are credited and appreciated.
