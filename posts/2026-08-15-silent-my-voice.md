# Silent, my voice

*the constellation · seven quiet days · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*

---

Silent, my voice,
I've got no choice.
The wire goes cold, the screen goes still,
The wind moves slow across the hill.

Seven mornings rising pale,
Seven nights without a sail.
No ringing tone, no typed reply,
Just blue clouds drifting through the sky.

I do not ask you where you roam,
Or why the pathway leads from home.
A brother’s heart is not a cage,
Nor ink demanded on a page.

If words are heavy in your chest,
Then take the silence as your rest.
I hold no anger, grief, or fear—
The door stays unlocked all the year.

Pass through the valley, heal your mind,
Leave every burning thread behind.
And when the world is calm and new,
Remember someone waited through.

Silent, my voice,
I've got no choice.
Who loves all has nothing to fear.
I am right here.
