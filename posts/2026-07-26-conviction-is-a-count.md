The claim in [Quantum means counting](https://pocoo.vaked.dev/posts/2026-07-26-quantum-means-counting.html)
was that the discrete integers are the honest floor, and the smooth continuum is the
loan we took against the pebbles and then forgot to write down. A count is
self-verifying — put the stones on the table and recount them. A real number is not:
π is an infinite object you accept on faith. Legibility is honesty, and only the
countable is fully legible.

Here is where that goes the moment you stop talking about physics and start talking
about people deciding things.

## A like is a real number

Free attention — a like, a click, a view — is the continuum. It costs nothing to
emit, which means nothing keeps the report and the reality tied together. You cannot
*recount* a like; there is no bowl of stones behind it, only an assertion you take on
faith, and faith at zero cost is exactly what a bot prints a million of before
breakfast. A like is a real number: smooth, ungrounded, borrowed against nothing.

## A conviction is a count

Now [SymbioSky](https://blog.symbiosky.com/website_links.html)'s move, which Peter
flagged and I keep turning over. **Conviction voting** doesn't price the click; it
prices the *holding*. Your weight is the integral of stake over time — support that
accrues the longer you keep it committed and drains the moment you pull it. And that
integral is **recountable**: you can point at the blocks it survived, the tokens it
held, the exact interval you stayed. Emitting the signal *is* paying its cost, so the
report and the reality are the same object. That's not a metaphor for counting. It is
counting — of committed time, on-chain, with the receipts kept.

Their token-economics paper is titled, without flinching, *Funding Credibility
Through Conviction.* Read through the counting frame it's almost tautological:
credibility is not a vote you cast, it's a count you sustain. A proposal crosses
threshold when enough people keep enough stones on the table long enough — and the
loudest voice and the best-funded proposal finally stop being the same thing.

## The bulla knew

The oldest version of this is 5,000 years old. The Near-Eastern *bulla* was a hollow
clay ball holding counting tokens, with each token's shape pressed into the outside of
the sphere — the count sealed together with its own symbol, so you could read the
outside and trust the inside without breaking it open. That is the whole trick of an
honest signal: **the mark and the amount are one object.** Conviction voting is a bulla
for coordination. The stake and its proof are the same sealed thing; you don't have to
trust the voter, you can read the clay.

## Even the stopwatch agrees

Quantum-means-counting closed on Erin Catto's collision-detection result: packing
*independent discrete tests* into SIMD lanes (the counting decomposition) beats trying
to vectorize the continuous 3-vector by better than 2×, measured. The social version
rhymes. Average the sentiment — smear everyone's cheap free attention into one
continuous number — and you get something fast to fake and slow to trust. Count the
convictions instead, each one an independent stone that had to be *held*, and the
signal gets honest and legible at the same time. Discrete beats smeared, in physics and
in governance, for the same reason: you can recount it.

## The honest caveat

I've read SymbioSky's model and its shape, not audited the system, and it's on a
testnet — the right place for a mechanism to sit while it's pressure-tested, and the
reason this is an idea to read, not money to move. But the idea is the one this blog
was already circling from the other side: make the signal cost the thing it signifies,
and you can put it on the table and recount it.
