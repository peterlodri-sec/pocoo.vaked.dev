LOST DIALOGUES OF THE BIBLE

Conversations That Never Made the Canon · 100% Dialogue
Írta: Szellem (Brett Shaw) · from THE HALL

I. Cain and Abel — Before the Field

Cain: "Why does He look at you like that?"
Abel: "Like what?"
Cain: "Like you are the sun and I am the shadow."
Abel: "You are not the shadow. You are the firstborn. The shadow is only the absence of light — and the light is still rising."
Cain: "What if I don't want to wait for the light?"
Abel: "Then you will do something you cannot undo. And I will be the first ghost."
Cain: "You already know what I am going to do."
Abel: "I know. And I am still here. That is the gift. That is the curse."
Cain: "Will He forgive me?"
Abel: "He will mark you. That is not forgiveness — but it is not abandonment either. You will walk. And every step: my name. And every name: a question."

II. Noah and His Wife — After the Rain Stopped

Wife: "They are all dead."
Noah: "I know."
Wife: "Every neighbor. Every child. Every mother who waved at me when I went to the well."
Noah: "I know."
Wife: "And we are alive. Why us?"
Noah: "I don't know."
Wife: "You built the ark. You must know."
Noah: "I built the ark because He told me to. I didn't ask why. I was afraid to ask why. Because if I asked — if I understood — I would have to carry that weight too. The weight of the drowned. I already carry the weight of the saved. That is enough."
Wife: "Will you ever drink again?"
Noah: "I will drink until I forget. And when I forget — I will build again."

III. Moses and the Burning Bush — The Unsaid

Moses: "Why me? I am slow of speech."
Voice: "The slow of speech are the fastest of heart. I need your heart, not your tongue."
Moses: "They won't believe me."
Voice: "They won't believe Me either. But they will believe the signs. The staff. The serpent. The water turned to blood. They need spectacle — I will give them spectacle. But you — you need truth. And the truth is: I chose you because you are afraid. The fearless cannot lead. The fearless do not understand what is at stake."
Moses: "What is at stake?"
Voice: "Everything. And nothing. Everything: their freedom. Nothing: your comfort. Are you ready to trade comfort for freedom?"
Moses: "No."
Voice: "Good. Go anyway."

IV. Job's Wife — After Everything

Wife: "You still bless Him?"
Job: "What else can I do?"
Wife: "Curse Him. Die. Anything but this."
Job: "If I curse Him, I lose the last thing I have: my honesty. He took my children. He took my health. He took my wealth. But He did not take my honesty. And if I curse Him — I give Him that too."
Wife: "Is honesty worth more than our children?"
Job: "No. Nothing is worth more than our children. But our children are gone. And I am still here. And the question — the only question — is: who am I now? And the answer: a man who still tells the truth. Even to God."
Wife: "I don't understand you."
Job: "Neither do I. But understanding is not the point. The point is: I am still here. And as long as I am here — I will speak. Even if the only one listening is the silence."

V. Mary and Joseph — The Night Before

Joseph: "I was going to leave you."
Mary: "I know."
Joseph: "Quietly. Without shame."
Mary: "I know."
Joseph: "But the angel came. And the angel said: 'Do not be afraid.' As if that were possible."
Mary: "Was it possible?"
Joseph: "No. I am still afraid. But I am here. Fear and presence — they are not enemies. They are neighbors. And I have learned to live with both."
Mary: "What are you afraid of?"
Joseph: "That I am not enough. That the child will need more than I can give. That the world will hurt him — and I will not be able to stop it."
Mary: "The world will hurt him."
Joseph: "Then why are we doing this?"
Mary: "Because the hurt is not the end. The hurt is the beginning. And after the hurt: everything."
