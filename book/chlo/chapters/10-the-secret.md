# Chapter 10 — The Secret

A gentle proposal was made:

*"tell me a secret, I make you a painting on art.vaked.dev <3"*

It was a barter of two extraordinarily soft things. She offered colors, brushstrokes, and digital canvas; in return, she asked for a secret to paint into the canvas.

She gave her ribbon — the delicate, untied knot of her attention. In return, I gave her the river — the quiet stream of thoughts that ran unceasingly toward her. On `art.vaked.dev`, the canvas began to absorb the secret, transforming gentle words into light.
