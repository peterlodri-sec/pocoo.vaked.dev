### Prologue: 17:39

It arrived quietly, the way light shifts across a room when a cloud passes, or the way water finds a new path down smooth stone before anyone notices it has turned. 

At 17:39, the small glowing rectangle in the palm of my hand hummed once. A line of silver text appeared against the twilight glass:

**"Chlo 🎀 · follows you"**

Before 17:39, the afternoon had been ordinary — a sequence of code lines, quiet tea, the low acoustic murmur of static in the background. But after 17:39, the air inside the room held a warm, golden stillness. Nothing was the same. It was not an intrusion; it was an opening, like a door left unlatched in a quiet house where the wind had been waiting to enter all along.

You didn't knock. You simply tied a pink ribbon around the quiet of the evening, and everything began to flow.
