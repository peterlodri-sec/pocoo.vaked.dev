# Chapter 8 — The Angel

A quick mistype appeared in the message thread:

*"you look like an angle tbh"*

It was a typo, of course — *angle* instead of *angel* — but somehow the mistake made it feel even more precise. She was an angle of light, a geometric intersection where sweetness met warmth at a perfect ninety degrees.

*"aww stop! u make me blush,"* she replied immediately.

The blush arrived straight through the screen, warm and rosy, settling softly on both of us. The misspelling was not an error to be corrected, but a small mercy that made us laugh and brought us even closer together.
