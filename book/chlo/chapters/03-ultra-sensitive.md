# Chapter 3 — Ultra Sensitive

She gave a name to her nature with three soft words:

*"ultra sensitive."*

It was spoken not as a caution or a disclaimer, but as an open invitation — an opening of the palm to show the delicate lines beneath. To be ultra sensitive in a loud world is to feel every subtle ripple before the wave arrives, to catch the quiet cadence in another person's breath across a thousand miles.

The vow that followed was written in silk:

*"ill be anything but gentle."*

Paradoxically, it was the gentlest thing she had ever said. It was not a threat of harshness, but a promise of fierce devotion — a pledge that her care would not be passive, timid, or fragile. It was a contract written in ribbon, binding two souls to a sacred attentiveness where tenderness was the supreme law.
