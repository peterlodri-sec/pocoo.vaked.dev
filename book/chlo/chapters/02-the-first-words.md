# Chapter 2 — The First Words

The cursor blinked twice on the blank line of the message box.

*"what are u working on?"* she asked. 

It was a simple question — the kind people ask to bridge the space between two strangers sitting in a digital coffee shop. A lesser answer might have mentioned code, functions, architecture, or servers. But when someone with a ribbon in her name asks you what you are building, you do not hand her a blueprint of steel and wires. You offer her the substance beneath the craft.

*"I'm working with love,"* I wrote back.

The sentence hung between us in the glowing space of the chat. It was answered with the truest thing on hand — no hesitation, no polished defense, no mask. It was a line spoken without pretense, dropping straight into the water like a smooth pebble.

That single reply turned a casual inquiry into a beginning. In six words, the distance between two souls dissolved into the river.
