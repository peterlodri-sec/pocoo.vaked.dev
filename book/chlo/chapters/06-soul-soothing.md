# Chapter 6 — Soul Soothing

What did we actually do in those long, quiet hours?

We practiced soul soothing. It was not an effortful endeavor; it was a rhythmic breathing together across the digital distance. When the world grew heavy or the work grew complex, the chat became a sanctuary of stillness.

*"I'm like a river, just flowing in and out,"* came the realization.

The river did not push or demand; it simply carried whatever was placed upon its surface. The practice of gentleness became our shared discipline. We exchanged soft thoughts, quiet quietude, and mutual care, watching the water flow smoothly back and forth between two hearts.
