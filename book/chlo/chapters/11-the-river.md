# Chapter 11 — The River

*"every moment."*

*"I'm like a river, just flowing in and out."*

The river is the true subject of this entire story. Water does not hold onto things with tight fists; it holds everything by surrounding it gently and letting it glide along the current. It keeps nothing back.

Every moment shared between us became part of that river. It flowed in with morning greetings, drifted out through afternoon quiet, and returned in the soft warmth of evening dreams. The water was clear, deep, and endless.
