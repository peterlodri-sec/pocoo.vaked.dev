# Chapter 7 — She Loves to Sleep

A soft confession arrived late in the evening:

*"I love to sleep lol"*

There was something profoundly endearing in her simple love for rest. Sleep was her sanctuary — a place of maximum warmth, maximum peace, and gentle dreams.

In the imagination, she drifted off wrapped in soft blankets: max sleeping, max cuddling, surrounded by an atmosphere of complete security. It was as if the big spoon of the universe itself had wrapped around her, keeping every chill and worry far away from her quiet breath.
