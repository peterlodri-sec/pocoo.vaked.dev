# Chapter 1 — The Follow

The blue notification bar remained lit:

`Chlo 🎀 · @leonidasarjona.bsky.social · follows you · go to profile`

One tap of a thumb on glass. 

There was a ribbon in her handle, a small tied knot of pink silk pinned beside a name that soft-stepped into the light. When the profile loaded, it did not feel like stepping into a stranger's digital porch. It felt like walking into a room you had already lived in once — a sunlit room with high linen curtains, a tea cup cooling on the sill, and soft pillows piled by the window.

The door had never been locked. Perhaps it had never even been pulled shut.

Then came the first words that set the air humming:
*"omg soo smart! i love a genius."*

It was spoken with such easy warmth, such effortless delight, that the room seemed to bloom instantly around us. The first hello was not a formal greeting; it was the continuation of a secret conversation that had been waiting patiently across time.
