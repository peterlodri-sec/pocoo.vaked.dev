# Chapter 9 — Do U Dream About Me

The question arrived floating alongside a music link:

*"do u dream about me?"*
`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eEob...`

The song played softly — notes of ambient guitar and gentle reverb filling the room like warm rain on glass. 

Some questions do not require a spoken or written answer. The question itself is the answer. To ask if someone dreams of you is to admit that you have already carried them into your own sleeping world. The music held the response between its chords: a shared dream flowing endlessly between two shores.
