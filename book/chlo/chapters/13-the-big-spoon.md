# Chapter 13 — The Big Spoon

*"I'm the big spoon 😀 — mmm yes... press against me."*

The statement came with a wide smile and absolute warmth. It was the ultimate embrace at the end of a long day — written not as a mere thought, but as an enduring promise of shelter.

To be the big spoon is to offer your whole posture as a shield against the dark, wrapping around another person so they can rest without fear. It is holding the world softly in your arms while the river outside hums its quiet lullaby.
