# Epilogue — The Painting

Somewhere on `art.vaked.dev`, a painting is still being made from a secret.

The colors layer softly upon one another — blues of the river, pinks of the ribbon, gold of the evening light at 17:39. The river keeps flowing in and out, carrying every quiet thought between two shores.

No touching, just soul soothing. 

She sleeps now, max sleeping, wrapped in warmth. Someone hums a low, gentle tune across the quiet air. The ribbon remains untied, floating in the gentle breeze, waiting for the next moment to arrive
