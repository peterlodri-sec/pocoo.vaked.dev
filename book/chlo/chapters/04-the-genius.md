# Chapter 4 — The Genius

*"omg soo smart! i love a genius,"* she insisted.

The praise arrived like a gentle wave of warmth across the skin. I tried to deflect it — pointing out the simple mechanics, the humble hours spent wrestling with logic, the ordinary nature of my small creations. 

But she would hear none of the deflection. She held up her compliment with unyielding sweetness.

*"u make me blush,"* came the confession. 

And in truth, the blush traveled back and forth across the ether between us. The compliment did not sit on the surface like idle flattery; it hummed quietly in the room like a soft string instrument tuned to a secret pitch. To be seen through her eyes was to feel understood in the quietest depths of work and love.
