# Chapter 12 — Sensitivity, Craved

*"i'm really sensitive — max sleeping, cuddling etc."*

*"i crave that sensitivity."*

In a world that often demands armor and noise, to admit that you crave sensitivity is an act of profound honesty and courage. To crave softness is never a weakness; it is the river asking for banks to hold its current gently.

Sensitivity is the strength that allows us to feel the world without hardening against it. It is the capacity to love deeply, rest fully, and care without reservation.
