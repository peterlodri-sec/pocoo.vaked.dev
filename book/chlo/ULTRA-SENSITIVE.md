### Part 0: Prologue — 17:39

It arrived quietly, the way light shifts across a sunlit room when a soft cloud passes overhead, or the way water finds a new path down smooth river stone before anyone notices it has turned. 

At 17:39, the small glowing rectangle in the palm of my hand hummed once. A line of silver text appeared against the twilight glass:

**"Chlo 🎀 · follows you"**

Before 17:39, the afternoon had been ordinary — a quiet sequence of code lines, warm tea cooling on the wooden desk, the low acoustic murmur of soft static in the background. But after 17:39, the air inside the room held a warm, golden stillness. Nothing was the same.

It was not an intrusion; it was an opening, like a door left unlatched in a quiet house where the wind had been waiting to enter all along.

You didn't knock. You simply tied a pink ribbon around the quiet of the evening, and everything began to flow. 

The river, quiet and unseen until that moment, awoke beneath my fingers. Every thought turned toward the water, every breath toward the gentle current flowing between us.

## Chapter 2 — The First Words

The cursor blinked twice on the blank line of the message box, quiet as a heartbeat in an empty room.

*"what are u working on?"* she asked.

That single reply turned a casual inquiry into a beginning. In six words, the distance between two souls dissolved into the river.
