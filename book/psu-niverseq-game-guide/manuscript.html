1. The World

The chaos overworld is a sacred-geometry universe: WoW's persistent zones and chat matrix, Diablo's dense polyhedral swarms, 90s platformer momentum, inside a Minecraft-like non-Euclidean space of tetrahedra, cubes, and icosahedra. The engine speaks in three symbols — the ternary wire {-1, 0, +1} — and the world is seeded from it.

The palette is fixed: neon cyan #00F0FF, synthwave magenta #FF007F, sacred gold #FFD700, emerald #00FF66, void #0B0F19. The bestiary is geometric: the cube-wolf, the icosa-owl, the dodeca-bear. You are born with a sacred name, and the lore unfolds from your root.

2. The Play

Three verbs carry the game: stomp, split, bounce. Stomp the critters to split them (Diablo's density); bounce off them (the 90s); collect the gold sparkles. The HUD counts your lives, your stomps, your splits, your sparks, your talent.

The deeper system is the hum: malas (+1), liberations (+2), god-nodes (+5), and butter lamps (+3) feed it. At 108 the ring blessing lands — "zero detection, zero pain" — six seconds of invulnerability and a pink ring pulse. Die during the hum and you respawn instead of dying. The 108 gates are the game's spine.

3. The ZEN

The chaos overworld's one original mechanic: stand still to charge. Stillness is a combat stat. Only a ZEN-stun can wound the deities — the gods cannot be hurt by motion, only by the pause. The game rewards the player who stops, breathes, and holds.

This is the doctrine made playable: coherence without collapse. The world permits everything to break except the distribution itself — and the player who stands still in the middle of the swarm is the distribution, holding.

6. The Engine

Under the floors: the 8b-is Engine. Rust 2024 core (wgpu, Rapier3D, SDF raymarching, bumpalo frame arenas, 64B cache-aligned entities), Go 1.26 network multiplexer (zero-copy sync.Pool, mmap ring buffers, NATS mesh), the dual-tier EventBus, the I/O HAL (everything since the 60s), Luau add-ons, the fauna five-layer system, the vaked-lsp editor gateway.

Open any floor in a browser. The controls: wasd move, space jump, stomp on the fall, T talents, N rename, s say, r reset. In PSU NIVERSEQ, be born with a name at the boot gate, let the lore unfold, and remember: the deities await — ultra-hard ZEN.
