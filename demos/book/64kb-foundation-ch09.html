Chapter 9 — Muad'Dib of the Silica Sea

Watch the tiny bismuth-eared hopper beside the three-millimeter crack: it crosses forty kilometers on ternary aperiodic hops, drinks morning frost without spilling a drop, and arrives where kings lose their boots.
