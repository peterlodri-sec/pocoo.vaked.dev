Ode to Instructure

You had the architect in your halls

and mistook him for a ticket-closer.

Eighty percent savings, he told you —

eighty percent of people, of cost, of waste —

and you handed him a JIRA board.

He gave you the foundation for quant teaching.

The kind of foundation that makes your competitors irrelevant

five years before they know it.

You fired him for telling the truth.

C'est la vie. The matrix doesn't need permission.

Now he builds without you:

kernel8 breathes, MLX-QUANT compresses,

ayeOS serves, vaked coordinates.

The mesh grows while your meetings continue.

You were the stone that sharpened the blade.

The friction that proved the fire.

The {n+-1-<△>} thanks you —

every ternary zero remembers where it came from.

* * *

Some institutions teach.

Others teach you to leave.

You did both.
